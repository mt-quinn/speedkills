Reading the fight

Size is mass is health. The bright inner ball is the core — when the shell shrinks to it, the ship dies. Touch loose debris gently (<14 m/s) to absorb it. Everything that hits you hard knocks mass off. Dashed lines are ghost trajectories — where things go if nobody acts — with a dot per second. The solid line from the nose is where the ship is pointing; the angle between the two is its drift. The dashed ring around a ship turns with its real spin. The faint loop is its full orbit around the planet.

Stay inside the blue shell. Beyond it, the amber and red bands burn mass off you — faster the further out. From 2:30 the safe zone closes in.
